Questions

What do I need to send you?

Your logo, photos, listing details, a short bio, your brokerage and license information, and any testimonials you have permission to share.

Who owns the domain?

You do. We register and connect it in your name, so it stays yours if we ever part ways.

Are edits included?

Starter and Standard include changes before launch. After launch, edits come with the Yearly Care plan, or can be quoted one at a time.

Do you run ads or set up tracking?

Not right now. Our packages focus on building the page itself.

What about brokerage and MLS rules?

We include the brokerage, license, and Equal Housing details you give us. You remain responsible for any approvals your brokerage or MLS requires, so check with them before launch.
